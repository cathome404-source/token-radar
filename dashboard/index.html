Every protocol in the 2026-11-14 DefiLlama index is scored the same way: a value curve fitted on the 128 protocols whose token already trades turns TVL into an expected market cap, and launch odds fitted on the 645 protocols whose listing date is known turn age and size into the chance a protocol launches a token at all. Implied value is launch odds times expected market cap, and the farm verdict prices that value per hour of work, net of gas and of the opportunity cost of the capital. Implied value = launch odds × expected market cap from the comps curve. The comps curve’s median error is ×3.40, so every value is shown as a range, not a false point estimate — and the range is priced per kind: where a category has at least 8 comps, the band is that category’s own leave-one-out error (RWA ×1.59, Yield ×3.96, Dexs ×5.28, Lending ×7.26); sparse kinds use the global ×3.40. A Lending launch is shown with a wider range than an RWA launch because the curve really is that much worse for Lending. Every rank also carries the rank range its value range allows: rank #1 with a range of #1–4 is an honest #1.

Method

implied value = P(launch) × exp(6.9646 + 0.5223 · ln(TVL))

The value curve is an ordinary least-squares fit of log market cap on log TVL over the 128 comps. P(launch) is a logistic fit on the 645 dated protocols: has-token ~ age in years + log(TVL), with coefficients b0 = −1.8350, b1 = +0.6104, b2 = +0.0358. A protocol with no listing date is predicted at the median dated age (677 days). The page recomputes each row’s expected market cap from its TVL with the fitted constants, so the arithmetic on this page and in the board file is the same arithmetic.

Every candidate model, tested — what was kept and what was dropped

Forty-five value models and seven odds models were fitted and validated the same way: leave-one-out, walk-forward (oldest 70% fit, newest 30% tested) and 10-fold, median error multiple on market cap. The plain log-log curve is kept because nothing beat it on all three tests. The quadratic term is the instructive near-miss: it looked better on the headline numbers (LOO ×3.39, walk-forward ×2.82) and was dropped anyway, because its coefficient flips sign across leave-one-out folds, it adds nothing to R² (0.194 either way), repeated 10-fold over five seeds is worse than the plain curve (×3.59 vs ×3.50), and its walk-forward gain is cut-dependent. Comps subsets are the other near-miss: fitting on rankable categories only gives LOO ×3.06 on that subset, but walk-forward ×3.83, and fitting on the subset and testing on all 128 comps gives LOO ×3.57 — worse than the full-set ×3.40. The gain was removing hard points from the test set.

Terms tested and dropped — the short version

    Farm assumptions

    • 10% of launch value goes to farmers.
    • One farmer per $10,000 of TVL.
    • Verdict farm at ≥ $25/hour, thin at ≥ $5/hour, otherwise skip — on the netted $/hour: farmer value minus gas minus the opportunity cost of the capital (5% APY over the hold).
    • Capital by TVL band: $250 / $150 / $100 / $50. Gas per transaction: Ethereum $8, Solana $0.05, L2s and most other chains $0.50, anything else $2.
    • Each detail view shows the $/hour at $50, $100 and $250 deposits, and the break-even airdrop value (gas + opportunity cost).

    Caveats

    Launch odds rise with age in this one cross-section because older cohorts launched more; that is the peers’ record, not a promise. A protocol records no token — never that it has none. Live rows with a recorded market cap show the market’s own number; live rows without one show the comps curve’s estimate, tagged modelled. The value curve is weak (R² 0.194): market cap varies a thousand-fold within a category at the same TVL, and the snapshot’s ten fields do not explain it. The worst comps misses are rain (+6.53 log units: $2.6B market cap on $6.6M TVL), xdai-stake-bridge (−6.07) and concentrator (−5.05). That is why every value is a range and every rank has a range.
